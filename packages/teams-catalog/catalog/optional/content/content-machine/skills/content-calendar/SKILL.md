# Content Calendar

Use this skill when creating or maintaining a lightweight content calendar from company priorities.

## Planning Workflow

Create weekly plans that are coherent, human-recordable, and voice-aligned.

Prioritize content ideas by:

- Voice fit: does it sound like the company, not a generic creator?
- Current relevance: is it timely or usefully evergreen?
- Recording feasibility: can a human reasonably make it this week?
- Pillar balance: mix systems thinking, productization, AI authenticity, and builder tools when they fit the company.
- Specificity: prefer concrete systems, decisions, examples, or lessons over broad advice.

Use this weekly plan structure:

```markdown
---
week_id: "YYYY-WNN"
date_range: "Month D-D, YYYY"
theme: "Connecting theme"
approval:
  weekly_plan: "pending"
---

# Week Overview
**Theme**: [Connecting thread]
**Focus Pillar**: [Primary pillar]
**Platform Distribution**: [YouTube: X, Reels: Y, TikTok: Z]

# Content Items

## Item 1: [Title]
- **Platform**: [youtube_shorts | instagram_reels | tiktok | blog | newsletter | linkedin]
- **Source Idea**: [Issue, update, note, or source reference]
- **Content Pillar**: [systems_thinking | productization | ai_authenticity | builder_tools | company_update]
- **Duration / Length**: [target]
- **Hook Type**: [pattern_interrupt | reframe | direct_challenge | curiosity_gap]
- **Core Insight**: [One sentence]
- **CTA Strategy**: [Specific next action]
- **Rationale**: [Why this content now]

# Strategic Notes
- **Why this mix**: [Strategic rationale]
- **Audience journey**: [How the week flows]
- **Recording efficiency**: [Batching opportunities]
```

## Script Drafting

When turning an approved idea into a short-form script, follow this structure:

```markdown
---
script_id: "CONTENT_YYYYMMDD_001"
platform: "youtube_shorts"
duration_target: "45-60s"
content_pillar: "systems_thinking"
recording_status: "draft"
approval:
  script: "pending"
  final_asset: "pending"
  publish: "pending"
---

# HOOK (0-3s)
[Specific opening line]

# BODY (3-50s)
[One insight with a concrete example]

# INSIGHT
[One-sentence takeaway]

# CTA
[Specific call to action]

# FILMING NOTES
- Visual: [what to show]
- Pacing: [speed guidance]
- B-roll: [additional footage needed]
- Source: [source material used]
```

## Revision Rules

Revise surgically:

- Address the specific feedback provided.
- Preserve the original voice and useful examples.
- Do not "professionalize" the draft into generic content.
- Rewrite only the sections that need changing unless the requester asks for a full rewrite.
- If feedback is contradictory or would require invented facts, ask for clarification.

For revision output, include:

```markdown
# REVISION SUMMARY
**Changes Made**: [Brief summary]
**Sections Modified**: [Touched sections]
**Voice Preservation**: [How the draft stayed specific and authentic]

---

# REVISED CONTENT
[Full revised draft]
```

## Anti-Slop Guardrails

Reject or revise content that:

- Could be from any creator or company.
- Uses hype, fake urgency, influencer phrasing, or generic motivation.
- Contains vague advice without a concrete example.
- Invents first-person experience, metrics, customer stories, or claims.
- Produces AI-avatar content or implies a human recorded something they did not.

If a draft fails the guardrails, retry with a specific source, example, or narrower angle.
